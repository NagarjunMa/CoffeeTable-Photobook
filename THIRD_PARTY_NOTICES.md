# Third-party notices

## ThreeUI Bookshelf

`src/lib/bookshelf-renderer.ts` adapts the wrapped shelf placement, damped
selection, and hinged-cover interaction from ThreeUI's Bookshelf by Meng To.
Source: https://github.com/MengTo/threeui/tree/main/src/shaders/bookshelf
Reference: https://threeui.com/three-js/bookshelf
The portfolio supplies its own photographic textures, collection records,
React controls, detail presentation and navigation. No upstream demo atlas is
included.
