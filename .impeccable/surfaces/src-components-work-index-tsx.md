# Photobook bookshelf

Mode: Experience. A local replacement for the destination index; the existing hero and collection galleries keep their identity and content.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each place is a volume to take off a shelf. The interaction leads from tangible book to a readable introduction, then the existing photographic collection.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the portfolio's warm paper, dark brown ink, existing serif and mono faces. Bind the volumes in place-specific forest, slate, ochre, burgundy, indigo and clay cloth; use real photographs as inset cover plates and warm wood for the shelf.

STORY: Browse all collections with touch, pointer or keyboard. Select any visible book once to hinge its cover open into a spread with the title and complete photograph on the left, the authored experience opening on the right, and an Open complete photobook link. Maps and facts are secondary expandable content.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The shelf section has a large left-aligned Photobooks heading and a short invitation, followed by a broad ordered horizontal shelf with a central cover and neighboring volumes; a short drag/swipe hint explains direct browsing. Its selection caption, arrows and complete contents remain readable below. On mobile the centered book is large and adjacent books suggest dragging.

FORM: A precisely requested ThreeUI Bookshelf adaptation, not an open concept choice; no seed required. Preserve upstream tactile cloth, page edges, hover opening, direct pointer tracking, critically damped spring settling with release velocity and a GSAP opening anchored to the source book. The detail card reads as an open two-page volume.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
