# Photobook carousel

Mode: Experience. Replace the destination bookshelf inside the established
portfolio. The photographer explicitly selected Skiper54 and confirmed that a
click should keep the photo-and-story preview before the complete photobook.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Explore places through the photographs themselves. Replace simulated
book covers with a centered, clipped-photo carousel that rewards curiosity.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit warm paper, brown ink, the existing Cormorant display face,
Inter reading text and olive navigation accents. Actual travel and wildlife
photography supplies the colour. No wooden shelf, cloth textures or fake spines.

STORY: Drag, swipe, use arrows or keyboard to choose a destination. Click any
visible photograph to read its authored opening beside the complete image,
then enter the full photobook. Escape and browser history return predictably.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A large Photobooks heading and short invitation sit above a
centered landscape-scale photograph with clipped neighbours. Below sit the
selected place, count, preview action and arrows, followed by destination links.
Mobile shows one generous photograph and a glimpse of its neighbours.

FORM: Precisely specified Skiper UI 54 adaptation; no concept seed applies to
this narrow, settled interaction. Embla owns the draggable track; GSAP expands
the selected clip mask with expo easing and reveals the caption and preview.
Reduced motion settles immediately. Preserve Thinking Orbs and image recovery.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
